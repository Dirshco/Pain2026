Poster 1: Digital Phenotyping of the “Invisible” Patient

A Computational Analysis of Nociplastic Pain Narratives on Reddit and TikTok

Patients with nociplastic pain often encounter medical skepticism, leading them to seek validation and community on social media. This retrospective, mixed-methods study analyzes the lived experiences of patients to identify narratives and coping mechanisms that can improve clinical communication and patient-centered care.

🔍 Key Findings

  • 100 high-engagement posts were analyzed across 8 condition-specific subreddits and 6 TikTok hashtags.
  • Reddit serves as a hub for peer education (79% of posts) and validation-seeking (32%).
  • TikTok normalizes the illness experience through humor/coping (34%) and day-in-the-life vlogs (58%).
  • POTS-related content drew the highest engagement on both platforms, highlighting significant unmet clinical needs.

Poster 2: Fibromyalgia & Chronic Illness mHealth Apps

Mapping the Gap in Digital Symptom Care (A Cross-Sectional Analysis of 168 Apps)
Presented by Dr. Noon Yousif and the Tele-Geriatric Research Fellowship Team

Effective fibromyalgia management depends on consistent patient self-tracking and energy pacing. This study evaluates the clinical usefulness, accessibility, and interface limitations of current mobile health (mHealth) applications supporting fibromyalgia and chronic pain patients.

🔍 Key Findings

  • Of 168 apps analyzed, only 9.5% (16 apps) are fibromyalgia-specific.
  • Most apps stop at basic data logging (e.g., journal entries, BP readings) without offering predictive insights or flare management.
  • 51.2% use a freemium model, and 36.3% include AI (primarily basic chatbots, not flare prediction).
  • Top barriers to sustained use include fatigue during flares, costly add-on charges, and app instability.
